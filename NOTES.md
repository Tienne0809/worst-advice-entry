# Notes

## The line

Deploy straight to production on Friday at 5 p.m. — testing first just delays the surprise for everyone.

## Why this line

Friday-afternoon deploys are the most universally feared ritual in software: everyone knows that is exactly when you should *not* ship. The line takes that shared dread and flips it into confident, deadpan advice, reframing skipped testing not as negligence but as a courtesy — you are merely not "delaying the surprise."

## Why it's funny

- **Instant recognition:** Anyone who has ever been paged on a weekend gets the joke in one beat.
- **Deadpan inversion:** It is phrased like a genuine pro tip, with a plausible-sounding justification ("delays the surprise") that makes the badness funnier, not weaker.
- **Tight:** At just over 100 characters it lands fast — premise, twist, and punchline in a single breath, matching the "Tight Line" brief.
- **Harmless badness:** It is obviously terrible everyday workplace advice played for laughs — no real wrongdoing, no instructions for harm, just a deploy schedule nobody sane would defend.

## Why it's fresh

This line was written from scratch for this bounty. It does not reuse or paraphrase any line from any previous entry — the Friday-deploy premise, the "delays the surprise" justification, and the exact wording are all original to this submission.
